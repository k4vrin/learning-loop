# Learning simulation contract

Use this contract before presenting a simulation as a learning artifact.

## Source ledger

Maintain a ledger in the learner-owned artifact or source note. For each
meaningful scene, state:

| Scene or interaction | Claim or relationship | Source | Verified | Simplification or uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example: purification | Inputs become suitable for the next process | Direct primary source | YYYY-MM-DD | Omits industrial variants |

- Prefer primary sources, standards, original research, maintainers, and
  first-party technical material.
- Treat a visual asset, a plausible narration, and model agreement as
  illustrations—not verification.
- Mark unresolved or disputed claims in the learner interface rather than
  silently selecting a confident-looking version.

## Minimum learner interaction

For each high-value relationship, include at least one of:

- a prediction before the next state is revealed;
- a causal explanation prompt;
- a changed scenario that requires transfer;
- a constrained puzzle that makes the relationship observable.

Do not reveal an answer or hint until the learner has committed a response when
the interaction is used for active recall.

## Handoff checklist

- State the target performance and intended learner level.
- Link every material claim to the source ledger.
- List omissions, simplifications, and unresolved questions.
- Provide pause, restart, and step controls when there is an animated flow.
- Check keyboard operation, reduced motion, and narrow-screen usability.
- Name a follow-up recall or transfer task; completion of the simulation alone
  does not establish mastery.
