# Rating rubric

- `Again`: The learner was blank, materially wrong, or could not reproduce the
  central rule without being told. Schedule in 1 day and reset repetitions.
- `Hard`: The core direction was present but important gaps, uncertainty, or
  hints remained. Schedule at least 2 days, then approximately 1.5 times the
  current interval.
- `Good`: The learner answered correctly without material assistance and handled
  the expected boundary. Schedule 4 days after the first success, then at least
  7 days and approximately double the current interval.
- `Easy`: The answer was accurate, fluent, and transferred to a changed context
  without assistance. Schedule 7 days after the first success, then at least 14
  days and approximately 2.5 times the current interval.

Use evidence, not confidence alone. Ask the learner to confirm the rating before
persisting it.
